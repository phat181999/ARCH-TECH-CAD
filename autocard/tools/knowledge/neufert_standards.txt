NEUFERT ARCHITECTS' DATA — KEY ERGONOMIC AND SPATIAL STANDARDS FOR ARCHITECTURAL DESIGN
Source: Neufert Architects' Data, 4th Edition (Ernst & Peter Neufert)

1. HUMAN BODY DIMENSIONS (ANTHROPOMETRICS)

1.1. Standing adult dimensions:
- Average male height: 1750mm; female: 1650mm.
- Shoulder width: 450mm (male), 420mm (female).
- Elbow height: 1050mm (male), 970mm (female) — counter/worktop standard height.
- Eye height standing: 1630mm (male), 1530mm (female).
- Arm reach forward: 750mm from body.

1.2. Seated dimensions:
- Seated eye height: 1140mm.
- Seat height: 430-450mm.
- Desk/table work height: 720-740mm standard.
- Minimum legroom under desk: 670mm height × 500mm depth.

2. DOOR STANDARDS

2.1. Residential interior doors:
- Minimum clear width: 800mm × 2100mm height.
- Standard: 900mm × 2100mm.
- Double doors: 1200mm-1600mm wide.

2.2. Entrance doors:
- Minimum clear width: 900mm × 2100mm.
- Accessible entrance: 1000mm minimum.

2.3. Sliding door track recess: 150mm into wall per panel.

2.4. Door swing clearance: minimum 600mm in front of door.

2.5. Recommended door heights by space:
- Standard rooms: 2100mm.
- Bathrooms: 2000mm minimum.
- Garage: 2200mm minimum.

3. STAIR DESIGN

3.1. Blondel's formula: 2 × rise + run = 620-640mm (optimal comfort range).
3.2. Residential stairs:
- Rise: 150-180mm (optimal 170mm).
- Run (tread): 250-300mm (optimal 280mm).
- Minimum clear width (1 person): 800mm.
- Comfortable width (2 persons passing): 1200mm.

3.3. Public/building stairs:
- Rise: 150-175mm.
- Run: 270-300mm.
- Minimum clear width: 1200mm.

3.4. Handrail height: 900-1000mm above nosing.
3.5. Headroom clearance: minimum 2000mm measured vertically above any stair nosing.

4. KITCHEN DESIGN

4.1. Work triangle (stove–sink–refrigerator):
- Each side: 1200mm-2700mm.
- Total perimeter: maximum 6700mm.
- Minimum side: 1200mm; maximum: 2700mm.

4.2. Counter dimensions:
- Work surface depth: 600mm standard.
- Counter height: 850-900mm (standard ergonomic height).
- Upper cabinet height above counter: 450-600mm clearance.
- Upper cabinet depth: 300-350mm.

4.3. Kitchen aisle widths:
- One-wall or galley (single worker): 1000mm minimum.
- Two workers simultaneously: 1200mm minimum.
- Island kitchen: 1000mm minimum clearance around island.

4.4. Minimum kitchen area:
- One-wall compact kitchen: 5.0 m².
- L-shaped kitchen: 7.0 m².
- Full eat-in kitchen with table: 12.0 m².

5. BATHROOM DESIGN

5.1. Shower:
- Minimum: 900mm × 900mm.
- Comfortable: 1000mm × 1000mm.
- Walk-in shower: 1200mm × 1000mm.

5.2. Bathtub:
- Standard: 700mm × 1700mm.
- Large: 800mm × 1800mm.
- Clearance at foot end: 600mm minimum.

5.3. WC (Toilet):
- Unit dimensions: 370-400mm wide × 600-650mm deep.
- Front clearance: 600mm minimum; comfortable: 750mm.
- Side clearance: 400mm minimum each side (accessible: 900mm one side).
- WC centerline from wall: 400mm minimum.

5.4. Washbasin:
- Width: 450-600mm; depth: 400-500mm.
- Installation height: 800-850mm.
- Knee clearance below: 650mm height × 500mm depth.

5.5. Minimum bathroom sizes:
- WC only: 900mm × 1200mm.
- WC + basin: 1200mm × 1500mm.
- Full bathroom (basin + WC + shower): 1800mm × 1800mm.
- Full bathroom with bathtub: 2400mm × 1800mm.

6. BEDROOM FURNITURE CLEARANCES

6.1. Single bed: 900mm × 2000mm footprint.
- Required clearance: 600mm one side, 750mm preferred both sides.
- Minimum bedroom width for single bed: 2600mm.

6.2. Double bed: 1400mm × 2000mm footprint (Queen: 1600mm × 2000mm).
- Required clearance: 600mm minimum both sides.
- Clearance at foot: 900mm minimum.
- Minimum bedroom width for double bed: 2800mm.

6.3. Wardrobe/closet:
- Hanging clothes depth: 600mm.
- Double rail: 1800mm height, each rail 900mm minimum length.
- Walk-in wardrobe: minimum 2000mm × 1800mm.

7. LIVING AND DINING AREAS

7.1. Dining table clearances:
- Seating space per person at table: 600mm width.
- Chair pull-back space: 900mm from table edge to wall.
- Circulation space minimum: 1200mm.

7.2. Sofa clearances:
- Coffee table in front of sofa: 400mm gap.
- Passage space between sofa and TV unit: 1800mm minimum.
- TV viewing distance: 1.5-2.5× screen diagonal width.

7.3. Minimum living room sizes:
- Small apartment (1-2 persons): 15 m².
- Standard apartment (2-4 persons): 20 m².
- Family home: 25-35 m².

8. ACCESSIBILITY (Universal Design)

8.1. Wheelchair dimensions: 700mm wide × 1100mm long; turning circle diameter: 1500mm.
8.2. Accessible ramp gradient: maximum 1:12 (8.3%); preferred: 1:20 (5%).
8.3. Accessible corridor: minimum 1500mm wide (1800mm preferred for wheelchair passing).
8.4. Accessible bathroom: minimum 2200mm × 2200mm; WC side clearance 900mm one side.
8.5. Grab bar height beside WC: 700-800mm; beside bathtub: 600-850mm.

9. PARKING

9.1. Standard car parking space: 2500mm × 5000mm.
9.2. Compact car: 2300mm × 4500mm.
9.3. Accessible parking: 3600mm × 5000mm (extra 1100mm side access).
9.4. Parking aisle one-way: 3000mm minimum; two-way: 6000mm minimum.
9.5. Garage single car: 3000mm × 6000mm (includes maneuvering).
